# Privacy Policy for ShootingLine (Draft)

> Template only—not a published privacy notice or legal advice. The current portal has no self-registration or in-app account deletion, and the scorecard and Ianseo integrations are not implemented. Do not publish until the developer identity and contact details are supplied, every data-collection and retention statement is verified against the deployed service, unsupported features and account-deletion claims are corrected, and the policy is reviewed for the applicable jurisdictions.

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

**Last Updated:** November 13, 2026

**ShootingLine** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the ShootingLine mobile application and web platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our digital scorecards, club management tools, and tournament dashboard.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Account & Profile Information:** When you register, we collect your name, email address, password (encrypted), bow division (e.g., Recurve, Compound, Barebow), and optional archery association member ID.
- **Scorecards & Performance Data:** Arrow values, end totals, round types, target distances, and personal best statistics that you log in the app.
- **Club & Roster Information:** Club affiliations, roles (archer, coach, club administrator), and event registrations shared within your joined clubs.
- **Synced Tournament Data:** Public tournament schedules, target assignments, and qualification/elimination results retrieved from external tournament systems such as Ianseo.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** App version, operating system, crash logs, and basic analytics used solely to maintain app stability and performance.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly to:

- Provide, save, and sync your digital scorecards and round history across devices.
- Display club rosters, shoot schedules, and member standings to authorized members of your archery club or association.
- Display live tournament leaderboards and target assignments.
- Send essential service notifications (such as password resets or tournament schedule updates).
- Diagnose technical issues and improve app reliability.

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers.**

---

### 3. Public vs. Private Data

- **Private Practice Rounds:** Rounds marked as personal practice are visible only to you unless you choose to share them.
- **Club & Tournament Results:** Scores submitted to a club shoot or synced from a public tournament feed (such as Ianseo) may be visible to other club members, tournament officials, or the public on event leaderboards.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

- We retain your account and scorecard history for as long as your account remains active.
- **Your Right to Delete:** You may request full deletion of your account and personal scorecard data at any time through the **Account Settings > Delete Account** menu inside the app or by emailing **[support@shootingline.app]**. Upon request, personal data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where official tournament records must be retained by your governing association.

---

### 5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit and secure encrypted storage for passwords and user records.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

ShootingLine is intended for general audiences and youth archers participating under club or parental supervision. If a user is under the digital age of consent in their region (e.g., under 13 in the United States or under 16 in the EU), account creation must be managed by a parent, guardian, or authorized club administrator.
