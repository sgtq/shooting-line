## One-Page Fact Sheet

- **Product Name:** ShootingLine
- **Tagline:** Scorecards, Club & Tournament Dashboard
- **Platforms:** iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play), and Progressive Web App (`shootingline.app`)
- **Target Audience:** Individual target/field archers, local archery clubs, and regional archery associations.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - **Digital Scorecard:** Interactive target face & rapid keypad entry, automatic end/round math, X-count tracking, and offline support.
  - **Live Tournament Sync:** Real-time target assignments, detail waves, qualification rankings, and elimination brackets synced with Ianseo tournament feeds.
  - **Club Management:** Member directory with bow class tags, shoot calendar publishing, and automated arrow-average & handicap analytics.
  - **Range-Ready Design:** Dual theme system featuring **Crisp Chalk (`#F8FAFC`)** for outdoor sunlight visibility and **Deep Forest Slate (`#1E293B`)** for indoor range halls.
